# Track 01: **prelude** (_in memoriam Malugo_)

## Musical Output

**Duration:** ~5 minutes

A contemplative string meditation that evolves from sparse, just-intoned harmonies (starting with a minor triad on D3) through increasingly complex chord structures (expanding to 7 notes), then contracts back to simple intervals. The texture combines sustained strings with extended techniques—harmonics, tremolo, glissandi—creating a shimmering, organic soundscape with occasional reversed samples and pitch-bending effects.

---

## Structure

**`01_strings/`** — Core harmonic layer. Progresses through 15 just-intonation chord structures over 5 minutes. Time-varying parameters control density (1-32 notes), instrumental variety (violin/viola/cello/bass), technique diversity (15 options), and timing irregularity. Notes placed every 5-1.5 seconds with spatial spreading.

**`02_runs/`** — Rapid scalar patterns derived from the chord data. Synthetic waveforms and samples create ascending/descending figures that weave through the harmony, increasing in speed and complexity.

**`03_tail/`** — Static coda. Quiet, breathy strings (non-vibrato, harmonics, air sounds) sustaining a simple 1:3/2 dyad across wide register.

**`04_grains/`** — Atmospheric clouds generated by granulating the strings recording (70-1500ms grains) with harmonic transpositions and synthetic tones, building density throughout.

**`05_tolls/`** — Ten irregular bell-like impacts: processed bass pizzicato with overdrive, sub-bass sine tones, and stretched plucked samples creating metallic sustains.

**`06_echoes/`** — Granular reflections reading backwards through the strings recording, gradually shifting from end to beginning as grains decrease in duration.

**`main.bell`** — Assembles all layers with metadata-driven timing, gain staging, reverb, and crossfades.
